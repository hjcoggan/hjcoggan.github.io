Red Cup Arcade: Privacy Policy

HJ Interactive · Effective 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Red Cup Arcade game for Android ("the game") handles, and why. HJ Interactive ("we") makes the game.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-up.
  • We don't collect, receive or sell any personal information.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to serve them, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings and progress on your device only. That includes coins, items, scores, stats, awards and the player names you type for Pass 'n' Play. None of it is sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

In the Tilt + Touch control mode, the game reads your phone's motion sensors to aim. These readings are used on the spot and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising

The game shows full-screen ads between games using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information from your device. This includes:

  • Your device's advertising ID.
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and app information.
  • How you interact with ads.

Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

We don't receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). The game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice later.
  • Ad personalisation. You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Deleting your data. Uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android settings, to delete everything it has stored on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
